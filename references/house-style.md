# House style (the default when there's no my-video-style)

A clean, professional default that works for most creators. The user's saved style always wins; this fills in what it doesn't say.

## Canvas
- Reels, TikTok, Shorts: 1080x1920, 9:16, 30 fps (keep 60 fps footage at 60 when the engine allows it).
- Feed posts: 1080x1350 (4:5). YouTube: 1920x1080 (16:9; vertical footage gets a blurred fill).
- Export: H.264, CRF 18 to 20, AAC 192k, `-movflags +faststart`.

## Safe zone (9:16 at 1080x1920)
Keep every word and card clear of:
- the top 220 px (status bar and app header)
- the bottom 450 px (caption, username, audio line)
- 35 px on each side
- the right 100 px from about two thirds down (like, comment and share buttons)

Scale these for other sizes. A style's own numbers replace these.

## The face
Never put text or graphics over the eyes-to-chin area. Use the space above the head, below the chin, beside the face, or behind the person (cutout). Check every beat in stills.

## Captions
- 2 to 4 words at a time, sentence case, no trailing punctuation.
- One bold sans (Inter 700 to 800, or the style's font), white with a soft shadow, or dark text on a light pill.
- One keyword highlight per phrase at most, in the accent colour. Fine to have none.
- Sit just below the chin or in the lower third inside the safe zone. Off when a card or effect puts text there.

## Titles and cards
- 2 to 5 words. Proper capitals. No em dashes or semicolons in on-screen copy.
- One accent colour for the whole video (default warm yellow `#FFD60A`), plus white and near-black `#111111`.
- Entrances: quick ease-out (0.25 to 0.4 s). Exits a little faster. No bouncy easing on everything.

## Pace
- Open on the strongest line. Something on screen within the first second.
- A new beat every 3 to 6 s. Never more than 2 layers at once. Leave breathers.
- 5 to 6 styles for the whole video. Same job, same treatment.

## Avoid
Glitch, neon, lens flares, particle bursts, shake, flash transitions and emoji, unless the user or a picked code asks for them. Fake numbers. Recreated screens shown as real ones. Private names, emails and numbers on screen.

## Sound
Sound effects on every visual beat, under the voice. Music only if asked. -14 LUFS, -1 dBTP.
